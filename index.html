UCL IEDE · Energy Use, Retrofit & Net Zero

Open data & models for a net zero building stock.

The catalogue of datasets and computational models from the Energy Use, Retrofit & Net Zero Research Theme at the UCL Institute for Environmental Design and Engineering. We study the energy use and carbon intensity of single buildings and whole building stocks to guide retrofit and speed up the move to net zero.
